## 2026-08-13 — clarify predecessor reach-back

The guidance distinguishes a retired tenure's advisory role from the availability
of its retained session history. Retirement alone does not impose a consultation
expiry. When a decision cannot be reconciled from artifacts, use the lineage's
exact resume handle and check the current runtime's ability to resume it.

The public lookup is `rig ask --help`: ordinary search returns recorded evidence;
`--wake` intentionally runs a bounded consultation. A missing history record,
unavailable runtime, timeout, or exhausted context can prevent an answer. Diagnose
that specific failure before inferring that all predecessor access is closed.

Treat the answer as testimony and disclose that the consulted tenure is retired.
Ask further questions when real work exposes a missing rationale. These are
instruction changes, not evidence that a successor read them or used the channel.
